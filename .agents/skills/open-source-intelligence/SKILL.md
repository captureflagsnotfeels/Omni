# OSINT

Define the intelligence question, lawful scope, time window, and collection limits. Minimize personal data and do not contact, deceive, harass, or attempt account access unless separately authorized.

Build pivots from known identifiers: domain/DNS/certificates/ASN, organization names, usernames, email patterns, document metadata, public repositories, archives, and public records. Record source URL, access time, publication/event time, query, and exact observed value. Prefer primary sources; corroborate material claims with independent sources and account for stale archives, reused handles, shared infrastructure, wildcard DNS, CDNs, privacy services, and data-broker errors.

Separate `FACT`, `SOURCE CLAIM`, `ASSESSMENT`, and `INFERENCE`. Treat matches as relationships with confidence, not identity proof. Do not expose unnecessary personal details in the report. Return the question, sources/provenance, verified observations, relationship graph or timeline when useful, alternative explanations, confidence, and collection gaps.
