# MITRE ATT&CK mapping

Map the behavior's objective and mechanism, not tool names or keyword resemblance.

1. Quote or cite the evidence-supported action: actor/process, action, target, context, and result.
2. Identify the tactic goal, then compare the behavior with candidate technique definitions and sub-technique scope.
3. Choose the narrowest supported mapping. Do not add lifecycle stages merely because they are common.
4. State confidence and why close alternatives were rejected. Separate attempted from successful behavior.
5. Record required and available telemetry, visibility gaps, detection opportunity, and validation.

Verify current technique identifiers and definitions against an available authoritative ATT&CK source when exact/current mapping matters. Output evidence, tactic, technique/sub-technique, confidence, rationale, alternatives, telemetry, and gaps. Omit unsupported stages.
