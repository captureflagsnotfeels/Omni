# Threat intelligence

Analyze IOCs, domains, IPs, infrastructure relationships, malware families, actors, and campaigns. Evaluate source recency, provenance, independence, and confidence. Separate `FACT`, `SOURCE CLAIM`, `ASSESSMENT`, and `INFERENCE`; do not treat a single vendor verdict as ground truth. Preserve lookup time and scope because intelligence changes. Use `open-source-intelligence` and `mitre-mapping`.
